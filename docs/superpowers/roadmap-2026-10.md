# Roadmap, October 2026 (car game)

Order agreed with the user on 2026-10-14. Each item is brainstormed (spec, plan) when its turn comes; v2.13.0 ships items 1 and 2 together.

1. **Day and night** - DONE on `feat/day-night` (spec/plan `2026-10-14-day-night*`).
2. **Car lights** - headlights lighting the ground, tail and brake lights, reverse lights (`2026-10-14-car-lights*`). Releases v2.13.0 with item 1.
3. **Camera control** - rotate the chase camera by touch; it returns behind the car when the car starts moving forward; when reversing the camera swings to the front of the car so the player sees what is behind it.
4. **Roads** - asphalt roads in the open terrain (join the start and the cities), with markings, higher grip; also gives the "asphalt" surface.
5. **Tyre dust** - physically plausible dust and spray from the wheels per surface (grass, sand, dirt, mud, asphalt, water): amount and colour from the surface, wheel slip, speed and sun/night light; needs a surface lookup (`game/materials.rs` weights, roads, mud near water).
6. **Realistic car physics** (tyres, suspension, transmission, drift), then flight, sling cargo and water (aircraft are removed from the game for now).

Update 2026-10-14 (late): the old delivery/race missions were removed (v2.14.0, free drive only, garage free). The rally mode (special stages with times) is a planned OPTION beside free drive; the stage runner, markers, HUD and results will be built for rally when item 4 and the roads exist.

Tech debt (user, 2026-10-14): the wind's side force on the car (a light push at speed in the open) is NOT done; the wind (direction/speed/gusts) is visible and audible only (dust, clouds, grass and tree sway, wind sound).

Tech debt (user, 2026-10-14): weather conditions (rain, fog, snow, wet surfaces) and the seasons (summer, autumn, winter, spring: foliage, ground colour, snow, grip) are NOT done; planned later.

Roads order (user, 2026-10-14): first A (stage relief: crests/jumps, ditches and banks, roadside props), then B (topology: junctions, side roads, city super-special loop), then the rally mode. Research on real rally stages first.

Tech debt (user, 2026-10-14): the autopilot's further improvement: machine vision (the car sees the road through a camera/depth view instead of reading the route) and self-training (the car learns by itself how to drive faster and steadier: reinforcement learning or numeric tuning of the style numbers over many simulated runs). Not now; the current autopilot (`game/autopilot`, styles in `rust/autopilot.json`) stays as the baseline and the reference driver for tests.

Tech debt (user, 2026-10-14): recording your own pace notes (the player records or edits the co-driver's calls for a stage); the generated notes with the system voice (R2, v2.23.0) come first.

## Tech debt after the driveline stages (2026-10-21, versions 2.72 to 2.75)

Left undone on purpose; each item is a candidate for its own small version.
- **Tyres and wheels:** trail-braking understeer is not modelled (the side force has priority while the tyre grips; a real tyre loses side grip under braking); the wheel's inertia, the slip peaks and the sliding shares are constants in `rust/src/game/wheel.rs`, to move into `surfaces.json` / `vehicles.json`; the tyre model of 2.77.0 (temperature, wear, cold pressure) has first-guess numbers, to tune by feel; the tyres are not carried between levels or kept in the garage, there is no tyre change, rain does not cool the tread, the tyre noise does not follow the grip.
- **Driveline:** the Peugeot's differentials are code (`Driveline::peugeot()`), not in `vehicles.json`; no spool / locked differential kind (an LSD with the full ramp is nearly locked); the active centre lock is a formula, not a tunable 3D map; the numbers are approximations of the real car (the sources name the layout only); the car menu's preset label does not follow a manual change of the parts.
- **Brakes:** the front/rear bias default is a constant (`wheel::BRAKE_BIAS = 0.62`) and the same for every car (the menu setting exists since 2.76.0); launch control has one fixed launch rpm (4800) and does not adapt to the surface; no handbrake bias or brake fade.
- **Gearboxes:** the sequential's idle creep is the automatic's; the box type and the automatic's character are not shown on the driving screen; no clutch overheating; no H-pattern grinding when the clutch is not pressed.
- **Engine and sound (older):** the tuning's turbo is not linked to the audio turbo whistle; the engine torque still comes from a table, not from the audio engine model; audio stage 4 (tyres on surfaces, water, rain, trees) not done; the shimmer of the hot air over the exhaust pipe is not done; ESC is not implemented.
- **Verification:** none of the 2.72 to 2.75 feel (shifts, wheelspin, ABS, handbrake, the differentials) has been seen on a device; the second phone's loading hang and the Settings crash are waiting for its log.
- **Performance (2026-10-21):** no render scale in the raster path (it draws at the native 1080 x 2400) and no adaptive governor for it; no depth prepass for the terrain; the night's headlight shading costs +3.5 ms; the models' fragment cost (trees, bushes, grass) is 5.5 ms; the hybrid render mode with soft shadows costs 15 ms more; the rain and the city scenes were measured only once.
- **Suspension (2.78.0):** the ride height and the dampers (bump and rebound) are not adjustable; the camber has no effect on the tyre's wear or temperature across the tread (a single temperature per tyre); the bars use the last substep's compression of the other wheel; the chassis presets are plain multipliers (no saved per-surface setups); the load sensitivity (0.12) and the camber optimum (-1 degree) are first guesses.
- **Heat and damage (2.79.0):** the oil and the turbo do not overheat; no warning light or sound for a hot engine or a burning clutch; no damage in the picture (a bent body, a broken lamp, a loose bumper) and none in the sound; the damage and the heat are not carried between levels or kept in the garage; a service is free and instant (no cost, no time); the first-guess numbers (the heat capacities, the 2.5 m/s threshold) are to be tuned by the player's feel; the hull of the city is the only thing besides props that registers a crash (terrain edges and the world limit do not).
- **Fuel and the stability control (2.80.0):** one fuel consumption model for every engine mode (no lean or rich mixture setting, no heat of the fuel); no low-tank warning or sputter; the only refuelling is the Service button (no pit stop, no time cost); the stability control has one fixed gain and no sensitivity setting or warning light, it ignores the handbrake and reverse, and it is not tuned for the autopilot (which drives with it off); the wheel naming comments in the older code (FL, FR, RL, RR for the indices 0 to 3) are wrong: the right wheels are 0 and 2, the left ones 1 and 3.
- **Interface (2.81.0):** the plain look covers the game screen and its panels; the start screen, garage, rally menu and the cluster and sticks are still in the SDK's Material 3 style; the shift pair's place beside the wheel is a guess, not tuned on a device; the exhaust smoothing time (0.2 s) is a first guess.
- **Autopilot (2.82.0):** it measures only asphalt and gravel (other surfaces take the nearest); it measures on a flat ground, so slopes come only from the route's own slope term; the daring loop has fixed rates and thresholds (first guesses, for the self-training of 2.84 to tune); the cross-track steering term (`STANLEY` 0.7 over a 0.8 m dead band) was found by a few trials and is a candidate for the training; the aggressive style is only slightly faster than the normal one; the sensors (2.83) and the self-training (2.84) are not done; a rare slide at a hairpin can still put the car 1 m beyond the edge.
- **Autopilot self-training (2.83.0):** it trains on two levels (2 and 21) and the car as built (no tuning, no weather), so the numbers may not suit another setup; no learned numbers per car (one set for all); the search box pushes lat_factor to its upper bound 1.8 and the margin to its lower bound 0.8 (the daring loop does the protecting): widening the box or adding the loop's own rates to the search is open; progress shows only as a percentage and the best cost (no lap-time readout); the training runs while the game runs (there is no pause or battery warning); the sensors of the old plan (surface and wetness ahead) are not done.
- **Max Attack (2.84.0):** trained for the Vulpes on two levels (2 and 21), other cars have the same numbers until the player teaches them; no sensors of the shoulder or of the roadside objects (it may hit trees at the edge); the landing after a big jump is not planned; slides are not tuned per surface (asphalt, gravel, snow); the flick is fixed (0.18 s, half lock) and is only a showpiece (it costs about 4 % of the time); there is no drift angle target or drift score; the rally mode and the city loop use the style like any other but were not trained for it.
- **Neural net driver (2.85.0):** it only copies a teacher (the normal style or Max Attack), so it cannot get far past it; a reward-driven fine-tuning (evolution strategies on the last layer, or a policy gradient) would need many short drives from random points of a stage (a start from a given route point and speed does not exist yet) and was not tried; one net, trained for the car as built, no tuning or weather in the inputs; no roadside sensors (rays) among the inputs; the weights file has no version, so a change of the inputs needs a new file name; the training takes three minutes of a busy phone and has no battery warning; the video of the rally (trackside, no controls) was only looked at, not learned from.
- **Reward training of the net (2.86.0):** the gain is modest (up to 8 % on one stage, about 1 % for Max Attack) and not repeatable on every level; only the output layer is searched; no per-car nets; the in-game run (30 rounds on 3 levels) is shorter than the host run (40 rounds on 6) and has no check against the old net before it replaces it (the best-so-far logic works inside a run); a later step could keep the old net when the new one is slower on a held-out stage; other ideas: the controller's labels improved by a search of 1 to 2 s ahead (expert iteration), training on several styles at once, all layers with many more drives.
- **Repertoire (2.87.0):** the rules are hand-written with fixed amounts and triggers; the net only copies them; not done: heel-and-toe, late apex, mid-bend brake tap, planned landing, drift-angle targets and a spin recovery (counter-steer when the car rotates past 60 degrees); the techniques are for Max Attack only.
- **Shoulder (2.88.0):** only the ground is judged (water, banks), not the rocks 4 to 8 m off the road, the signs or the spectators' places; the width is up to 2 m, whatever the real shoulder is; one level out of six has a low-speed slide that takes the car 7 m wide (a recovery rule did not help, the cause is not found); the learned nets must be taught again to copy the new line.
- **Vision net (2.89.0):** 24 % and 11 % slower than its teacher on unseen stages; the rays do not see trees, rocks and signs; the net is small (48 x 48) for 51 inputs and the loss does not fall with more data: a bigger hidden layer (a new format with the size), a short memory (the last few frames: the accelerations are only one step) and the reward training of the vision net are open; the coarse hint of the route is still given; the city part (junctions) was not tested; the training takes about ten minutes of a busy phone; a net that drives with no route at all (a free ride on any road) is a step beyond.
