# Garage (v2.11) — design

Status: approved in chat (2026-10-14). The user supplied a Sketchfab Vulpes WRC (231k triangles, 100 MB, a game rip of unclear licence) as a reference; the car model is generated by code with matching proportions instead of using the file.

## 1. Goal
A garage: a second car (Vulpes WRC) that can be bought, four upgrade categories for every car, a garage screen with a turntable 3D preview, and the selected car used in free drive and in ground missions. Aircraft are untouched.

## 2. Cars and upgrades
- `vehicles.json` cars gain optional `model` ("suv" default, "rally"), `price` (money, 0 = owned from the start) and `upgrade_cost` (base price of one upgrade level). `offroader`: price 0, upgrade_cost 300. New `vulpes`: model "rally", price 3000, upgrade_cost 750, mass 1230, size 1.78 x 1.44 x 3.96 m, mounts x +-0.76, y 0.10, z +-1.225, rest 0.32, radius 0.32 (centre of mass 0.54 m above the ground, rollover limit 1.41 g above its grip 1.2), spring 32000, damper 3200, engine_force 14000, brake_force 14000, max_speed 42, steering a little quicker, lighter aero drag.
- Upgrades, levels 0..3 each: **Engine** (engine force +8/+16/+25 %, max speed +3/+6/+9 %), **Tyres** (grip +5/+10/+15 %), **Suspension** (spring and damper +10/+20/+30 %), **Brakes** (brake force +20/+40/+60 %). A level costs `upgrade_cost * (1, 2, 4)`. Pure functions in `game/garage.rs`: `apply(spec, levels) -> VehicleSpec` (only those numbers change), `upgrade_price(spec, level_to_buy)`.
- Rules (profile methods): a car must be owned to be selected or upgraded; buying needs enough money (money is reduced exactly by the price); levels cap at 3; buying twice or without money changes nothing and reports false.

## 3. Profile
`garage: { selected: "<id>", cars: { "<id>": { engine, tyres, suspension, brakes } } }` (a car appears when owned). The profile file version stays 1 (the field is optional): an old file loads as only the off-roader owned and selected, no upgrades. Unknown car ids in a file are dropped on load; a selected car that is not owned falls back to the off-roader.

## 4. Game and renderer
- `World` carries the car spec (`World::new_with(seed, spec)`, `for_mission_with(m, spec)`; `new`/`for_mission` keep the off-roader for tests); `Game` builds every world with the selected car (profile) including upgrades.
- The renderer rebuilds the car body and wheel meshes when the vehicle's model style, size or look changes (`Raster` compares a key each frame).
- Garage scene: `Game::start_garage(car_index)`: a world on the flat spawn plateau with the car standing still and a camera orbiting it (about 20 s per turn, 6.5 m away, 1.6 m up); `gameGarageCar(index)` switches the shown car. No input.
- New model style "rally" (`build_rally(spec)`): a hatchback body lofted from stations (nose, hood with vents, windscreen, roof with an air scoop, tail), widened wheel arches, front bumper with a large intake and four rally lamps, rear wing on two stands, side mirrors, side exhaust, rally wheels (the existing wheel builder with a bigger rim); white body with red and blue accents from the `look` colours; at most 9000 triangles in total.

## 5. UI (Kotlin)
`GarageActivity`: the preview fills the screen (a SurfaceView running the game engine in garage mode); a right-hand panel with the car name, previous/next arrows, a Buy (price) or Select button, four upgrade rows (name, level pips, price, a Buy button, disabled when maxed or unaffordable) and the money at the top; a Back button. Entry in the main menu. JNI: `garageInfo(path) -> FloatArray` (`[money, selected_index, car_count, then per car: owned, price, upgrade_cost, engine, tyres, suspension, brakes]`), `garageBuyCar(path, index)`, `garageBuyUpgrade(path, index, upgrade)`, `garageSelect(path, index)` (each loads, changes, saves and returns a boolean); names by index in Kotlin (EN+RU).

## 6. Testing
Host tests: params parsing of the new fields and the Vulpes numbers, the centre-of-mass guard for every car, the Vulpes does not roll in hard turns and out-accelerates the off-roader, upgrade maths (only the intended fields change, monotonic, level 0 identity), prices, buy/select rules (money, cap, ownership), profile round trip and old-file compatibility, unknown ids, the garage world and camera orbit, model budget and dimensions (bounding box within 6 % of the spec size, wheels at the mounts, normals unit and outward, at most 9000 triangles, every material present). Device: screenshots of the garage with both cars and of driving the Vulpes; handling feel is judged by the user. One final review on the most capable model.

## 7. Out of scope
Paint or decals, aircraft in the garage, used cars, the turntable manual rotation by touch, more than two cars, NPC orders.
