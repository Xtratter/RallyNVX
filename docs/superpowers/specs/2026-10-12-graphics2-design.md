# Graphics II: nature, sky, water, buildings (v2.6) — design

Status: draft for review. Date: 2026-10-12. Builds on v2.5 (material textures, model pipeline, tracks).

## 1. Goal and decisions

Second half of the look upgrade planned in the Graphics I spec. The user chose **everything at once, but simpler**
(one release, each part with a reduced scope) and **water for looks only: shallow, drivable**. Constraints are those of
Graphics I: no external assets (all meshes/textures generated by code, deterministic per seed), raster mode C only,
phone at 60 fps or better on High (120 fps now in cities), quality levels scale the cost, EN+RU docs, checks on the
phone (no CI screenshots). Gameplay physics, missions and collision shapes do not change.

## 2. Plants and rocks

- `raster/nature_models.rs` builds generated meshes in the existing model vertex format (`vehicle_models::Mesh3`, same
  material ids): 4 trees (2 conifers, 2 broadleaf: lofted trunk plus 3-5 crown layers/ellipsoids), 2 bushes, 3 rocks
  (noise-displaced icosphere, flat-faceted). Colour varies per instance by a hash of the position.
- Props keep their gameplay data (`Prop{kind,pos,radius,height}`); the renderer picks a variant from the position hash
  and scales it to the prop's radius/height. Collision is unchanged. Triangle budget per variant: trees <= 400,
  bushes <= 150, rocks <= 120.
- Instancing through the model pipeline (one draw per variant, per-instance matrix + tint); the instance buffer grows
  to a documented cap (props within the draw distance only, nearest first).
- Grass: tufts (crossed quads, 6 triangles) placed on a camera-centred grid within 40 m on grass-weighted ground
  (`terrain_weights` grass >= 128, slope low, not in cities or on water), up to 3000 per frame, wind sway in the
  vertex shader. Quality: Low 0, Medium 1500, High 3000.

## 3. Sky and light

- `fs_sky`: procedural clouds (2D fbm on a plane projection, drifting with time), sun disc and glow, softer horizon
  haze; one shared `sky_gradient` function so terrain/model reflections use the same colours.
- Time of day is dynamic: the sun moves during play along a day arc (full cycle in 12 minutes of play, the starting
  phase comes from the level seed). The elevation swings between 10 degrees (dawn/dusk, warm and long shadows) and 60
  degrees (noon); sky, fog, light and ambient colours are interpolated from the elevation. No night in this release
  (the sun never sets; the cycle goes dawn - noon - dusk - noon). The shadow map follows the sun direction. The cycle
  length and the elevation range are constants in `game/daylight.rs` (pure, tested) and `WorldView.sun/daylight`
  carry the result; paused/menu time does not advance it.
- Models get a fresnel rim and a sky reflection term on paint, glass and chrome (deferred from Graphics I).

## 4. Water

- Lakes fill low ground: the level has one water height chosen from the seed (the 12th percentile of sampled terrain
  heights, flat plateaus under cities excluded). `Terrain`/`World` expose `water_level() -> Option<f32>`.
- Rendering: for every terrain tile whose lowest vertex is below the water height the renderer builds a flat water
  mesh at that height (same grid as the tile, coarse LOD only is enough), with a per-vertex depth
  `water_level - terrain_y` (clamped at 0). Drawn after the terrain with alpha blending and depth test, no depth write.
  Fragment shader: alpha and colour from the interpolated depth (clear and light at the shore, darker and opaque in
  the deep), two scrolling noise layers for wave normals, fresnel mix of sky colour and water colour, sun glint, fog.
- Gameplay: ground vehicle in water (terrain height below the level at the wheel): rolling drag factor up
  (`vehicles.json cars.*.water_drag`) and splash particles (cube instances) while moving; aircraft are unaffected.
- Mission goals, rings, pads and the start are never placed in water (same filter style as `clear_of_buildings`).

## 5. Buildings and city ground

- 4 facade palettes chosen per building by a hash; cornice and parapet boxes on the roofs; window glass takes a sky
  reflection term.
- Street surface is even asphalt: a dedicated clean `Asphalt` look (no grass/dirt blend, no noise bumps: the city
  plateau is already exactly flat, the surface draws as one smooth dark-grey material with fine grain), painted lane
  marks and edge lines (generated, tiled road-mark layer in the same array: layer count 5 -> 6), kerbs along the
  blocks. Drawn on the plateau in the city area only; the terrain texture shows again outside the city rim.
- Street lamps along the road edges (cheap instances, within the city draw distance).

## 6. Testing and verification

Host tests: variant meshes (finite, unit normals, outward, triangle limits, bounded size), variant choice deterministic
and stable per position, grass placement (no water/city/steep/rock, count cap, grid determinism), water level from a
seed (stable, below the highest terrain, none on flat seeds), mission goals never in water over many seeds, time of day
from the seed, water drag in `vehicle` tests. Device: build locally, install over root, launch GameActivity with
extras (city, mission), screenshots of forest, lake, city, sky, flight; fps at High >= 60. One final review on the most
capable model; findings fixed with RED->GREEN.

## 7. Vehicle stability (ships first as v2.5.1)

The car tips over too easily. Cause (measured from `vehicles.json`): the centre of mass sits at the suspension
attachment height, 1.05 m above the ground (`rest 0.6 + radius 0.45`), half-track 0.95 m, so the rollover limit is
0.95/1.05 = 0.9 g, below the tyre limit (`grip 1.1`): a hard turn rolls the car before it slides. Fix: lower the centre
of mass relative to the wheels by moving the attachment points up in body space (`wheels[*].y = 0.35`, so the CoM is
0.70 m above the ground; rollover limit 1.36 g > grip, it slides first). Consequences handled together: the body and
wheel poses (`vehicle_models::car_body_pose`, `car_wheel_poses`) follow the new attachment height so the look does not
change; scrape height, reset height and the roof/hull checks are re-derived from the spec; no other number changes.
A host test drives a hard steady turn at several speeds and asserts the car never rolls over on flat ground, plus a
test that the CoM height is below the rollover limit implied by `grip` (guards future edits of `vehicles.json`); the
existing vehicle tests must still pass; before/after rollover speed is reported. Released as **v2.5.1** before the
graphics work; docs/vehicles.md documents the relation.

## 8. Out of scope

Night, rain/snow, real reflections or refraction, swimming/boats/bridges (later with fords), shadows from grass,
LOD meshes for trees (distance culling only), new vehicles, UI changes.
