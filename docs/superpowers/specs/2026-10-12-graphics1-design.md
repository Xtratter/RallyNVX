# Graphics I: vehicles and ground (v2.5) — design

Status: draft for review. Date: 2026-10-12. Builds on v2.0-v2.4 (raster renderer, vehicles.json, cities).

## 1. Goal and decomposition

The user asked whether the look is a prototype (yes: stylised low-poly from primitives) and chose to improve vehicle
models, ground materials, plants/rocks/buildings and sky/light/water, after the city missions and before the rescue
stage. The work is split in two releases: **v2.5 Graphics I** (this document: vehicle models and ground materials,
object lighting) and **v2.6 Graphics II** (plants, rocks, buildings, sky, soft light, water). Later: rescue v2.7,
garage and NPC orders v2.8, render modes A/B v2.9, polish v3.0.

Constraints: no external assets (everything is generated by code, deterministic); the raster mode C stays the target
(modes A/B come later); the phone must stay at 60 fps or better on High quality; quality levels scale the cost.

Assumptions (editable): stylised-realistic look (clean forms, believable materials, no photoreal claims); the city
tiles and buildings keep their flat colours in this release.

## 2. Ground materials

### 2.1 Material maps (`raster/texgen.rs`, pure)
- Five tileable 256 x 256 RGBA8 maps generated once at startup from fixed seeds: grass (two tones with noise and
  small tufts), dirt, sand, rock (layered strata), asphalt (kept for the city later). Each has a full mip chain
  (box filter) and is stored in one `texture_2d_array` (5 layers, 9 mips). Colour space sRGB.
- `texgen::make(kind, size) -> Vec<u8>` (RGBA, tileable: the left/right and top/bottom edges continue each other),
  `texgen::mips(level0, size) -> Vec<Vec<u8>>`.
- The sampler repeats, linear with mip filtering, anisotropy up to 4.

### 2.2 Per-vertex weights (`game/materials.rs`, pure)
- `terrain_weights(h: f32, normal: V3, x: f32, z: f32) -> [u8; 4]` returns weights for (grass, dirt, sand, rock),
  summing to 255:
  - rock: slope steeper than about 35 degrees (`normal.y < 0.82`, smooth transition) and high ground;
  - sand: the lowest heights (below about -4 m) with a soft edge;
  - dirt: noise patches (hash noise over 24 m cells, smoothed) plus moderate slopes, and bare spots on grass;
  - grass: the rest.
- `Terrain::tile_weights(tx, tz, lod) -> Vec<[u8; 4]>` matches `tile_mesh` vertex for vertex (skirt vertices copy the
  weights of their parent). Weights are stored in the terrain vertex buffer next to position and normal (Unorm8x4).

### 2.3 Terrain shader
- Four samples of the array (one per material) at world UV `wp.xz / 6 m`, one macro sample at `wp.xz / 40 m` that
  modulates brightness to hide repetition, blended by the interpolated weights; lit as before. No noise is computed
  per pixel.

### 2.4 Wheel tracks
- `game/tracks.rs`: a ring buffer of up to 256 track segments; a wheel in ground contact at speed > 2 m/s adds a
  segment every 0.35 m of travel (position on the ground, direction, width 0.3 m). Old segments are overwritten.
  Drawn as dark thin quads through the existing instance pipeline (a 0.03 m offset above the ground, aligned to the
  terrain normal). On sand and grass only (not on rock slopes and city tiles).

## 3. Object lighting and the model pipeline

- A new pipeline `model` for vehicles: vertex = position, normal, colour (RGB) and material id (packed in alpha);
  instance = model matrix (per part), tint. Materials: 0 paint, 1 glass, 2 rubber, 3 plastic, 4 chrome, 5 emissive.
- Fragment shading adds, on top of the existing lighting: a sun specular lobe, a sky reflection (the analytic sky
  gradient of `fs_sky` factored into a WGSL function used by both), a fresnel rim for paint and glass, and emissive
  for lights. Rubber is matte. The model pipeline also draws into the shadow map.

## 4. Vehicle models (`raster/vehicle_models.rs`, pure)

Meshes are generated from parameters (no files); the sizes come from `vehicles.json` (the new optional `look`
section holds colours; physics is untouched).
- **Off-roader (<= 4000 triangles):** lofted body (about 12 cross-sections along the length with a sloped hood, fender
  arches), side and front glass, bumpers, headlights and tail lights (emissive), grille, roof rack, spare wheel on the
  rear door. Wheels are separate parts (tyre with tread blocks, rim with bolts) that steer and spin.
- **Helicopter (<= 2000 triangles):** teardrop fuselage, cockpit glass, tail boom and fin, skids, rotor hub and blades
  (the blades spin; at high speed the disc is drawn with a blurred translucent-looking tint), tail rotor.
- **Drone (<= 1000 triangles):** body, four arms, motors, four propellers (spinning), a gimbal camera, legs.
- `build_offroader(spec, look) -> ModelSet`, `build_heli(spec, look)`, `build_drone(spec, look)` return parts
  `{ mesh: Mesh3, tint, role }` (role = body, wheel(i), rotor...). `Mesh3` has `verts: Vec<[f32; 10]>`
  (pos, normal, colour rgba) and `idx: Vec<u32>`.
- `vehicles.json` gets `look` per car/aircraft: `body_color`, `accent_color`, `glass_tint`, `wheel_width`; defaults
  exist when absent, invalid values give a readable error like the rest of the file.

## 5. Testing

Host tests (no GPU), failing first:
- texgen: deterministic, tileable edges (max edge difference below a threshold), colour ranges per material, mip
  chain sizes and averages, no NaN, each map non-uniform.
- terrain_weights: sums to 255, flat and low ground is grass, steep ground is rock, the lowest heights have sand,
  continuity along a slope (weights change smoothly), determinism; `tile_weights` length equals the mesh vertex count
  for all LODs.
- tracks: spacing 0.35 m, cap 256, nothing in the air or below 2 m/s, nothing on rock slopes.
- model builders: indices valid, normals unit, size within 5 % of the spec dimensions, triangle limits, every part
  has a material id and a role, wheel parts are centred on their axles, rotor parts centred on the hub.
- vehicles.json `look`: parses, defaults, errors.
- Shaders validate in naga (terrain and model pipelines).
- CI smoke: desktop screenshot with the car and terrain (`screenshot-game.png` shows textured ground and the model).
- Phone check (owner's permission): FPS on High; the target is at least 60 fps.

## 6. Risks

- Texture array and mips support: fine on Vulkan; sampler anisotropy is optional.
- Frame cost of five samples plus the model pipeline and track quads: small, measured on the phone.
- Visual taste: colours and proportions need a human look; they live in `vehicles.json` and `texgen`/`materials`
  constants so they are cheap to tune.
- Scope: no plants, buildings, sky or water here (v2.6).
