# Procedural Sound Implementation Plan (v2.8)

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:executing-plans (inline).

**Goal:** procedural engine/rotor/wind/event sounds on Android via AudioTrack.
**Architecture:** pure `audio` module (dsp, synth, director) tested on the host; thin glue (global synth, JNI, Kotlin AudioEngine).
**Tech Stack:** Rust (no new crates), Kotlin AudioTrack.
**Spec:** docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-12-sound-design.md

## Global Constraints
- No files/assets, no new dependencies, mono 44.1 kHz PCM16; version 2.8.0 (versionCode 19); EN+RU texts; trailer `Co-Authored-By: Claude Sonnet 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`; device checks short with Termux in front at the end; never touch the user's profile.json.
- The game loop must not block on audio: only short `Mutex` sections; the audio thread never allocates in `render`.

## Review Focus
- Real-time safety: no panics/NaN in `render` (a panic in the JNI audio thread must not kill the game), lock hold times, behaviour when the buffer is empty or odd-sized.
- Pause/mute/focus loss and activity lifecycle: no sound after leaving the game, no leaked thread/AudioTrack, restart after resume.
- Director edge cases: first frame after a new level/restart (no spurious events), event repeats while a condition persists, free drive (no mission), aircraft vs car switch.
- Loudness: sum of voices never clips, events do not bury the engine.

### Task 1: DSP and synth core
**Files:** create `rust/src/audio/{mod,dsp,synth}.rs`, `rust/src/audio/synth_tests.rs`; modify `rust/src/lib.rs` (`pub mod audio;`).
**Interfaces:** Produces `audio::{RATE, Machine, Surface, AudioParams (Default), Event, Synth}`; `Synth::new()`, `set_params(&AudioParams)`, `push(Event)`, `set_muted(bool)`, `render(&mut [i16])`; `engine_freq(rpm: f32) -> f32`.
- [ ] Tests (RED first, in `synth_tests.rs`, with a Goertzel helper `power(buf, freq)`): output finite and within i16 for 10 s of pseudo-random parameter sweeps over every machine; `engine_freq` strictly increasing; a car render at rpm 0.2 has more energy at `engine_freq(0.2)` than at `engine_freq(0.8)` and vice versa for rpm 0.8 (roll, slip, wind = 0); `active = false` or `set_muted(true)` fades to exact silence within 0.1 s and `set_muted(false)` brings sound back; `Event::Checkpoint` raises energy at 660 Hz within 0.3 s on a silent synth and the output is silent again after 1 s; 20 pushed events never exceed 8 voices (`voices()` accessor) and never clip; identical sequences of calls give identical buffers (two synths).
- [ ] Implement: `Noise` (xorshift32 to [-1,1]), `OnePole` low-pass, `Smooth` (exponential), phase-accumulator oscillators; engine = saw + square stack with six harmonics at `engine_freq(rpm) = 25 + 110*rpm`, amplitude `0.12 + 0.2*load`; rolling noise (ground cutoff 1.5 kHz, water 0.6 kHz plus slow gurgle), squeal (band-pass by two one-poles around 1.1 kHz) from `slip`; heli rotor: 55 Hz tone and noise bursts at the blade-pass rate `13 + 4*rpm` Hz; drone: four tones around 300-420 Hz scaled by rpm; wind: low-passed noise; voices: struct with kind, age, params, envelope; mix, `tanh` soft clip at gain 0.9, master fade 50 ms for mute/inactive; all parameters smoothed with a 80 ms time constant. `render` clamps and converts to i16, no allocation (fixed arrays).
- [ ] `cargo test --lib audio`; commit `feat(audio): procedural synth`.

### Task 2: Director
**Files:** create `rust/src/audio/director.rs`, `director_tests.rs`.
**Interfaces:** Produces `Director::new()`, `update(&mut self, w: &World, throttle: f32, active: bool) -> (AudioParams, Vec<Event>)`. Consumes `World` fields: `vehicle`, `aircraft`, `air`, `mission`, `terrain`, `run_info()`.
- [ ] Tests (scripted worlds): free drive at rest gives `rpm` low and no events; driving gives `rpm` rising with speed/throttle and `roll > 0`; a flooded terrain (`Terrain::flooded`) gives `surface == Water` and one `Splash` on entry only; a drop from height gives one `Touchdown` with strength proportional to speed and nothing while grounded; abrupt horizontal stop gives `Crash`; air missions: heli `Machine::Heli`, drone `Machine::Drone`, landed rpm 0.45, flying higher rpm, `wind` rises with height; `Touchdown` when Flying -> Landed once; `next_index` increase gives `Checkpoint` once; rescue boarding gives `Board` once per increase; outcome Passed/Failed gives exactly one `Success`/`Fail`; `active = false` gives `active == false` and no events; a world switch (new `World`, different seed) produces no spurious events on its first update.
- [ ] Implement per the spec §4 (store previous values: grounded, wet, status, next_index, loaded, outcome flag, previous horizontal speed, seed).
- [ ] `cargo test --lib audio`; commit `feat(audio): director`.

### Task 3: Glue (JNI, game loop, Kotlin)
**Files:** `rust/src/audio/mod.rs` (global `static SYNTH: Mutex<Synth>`, `pub fn feed(params, events)`, `pub fn render_into(&mut [i16])` which never panics), `rust/src/game/app.rs` (`Game` owns a `Director`; in `frame` call it after the steps and `audio::feed`), `rust/src/game_jni.rs` (`Native_audioRender(ShortArray)`, `Native_audioMute(boolean)`), `app/src/main/java/dev/starinin/raytrace/{Native.kt, AudioEngine.kt, GameActivity.kt}`.
- [ ] Tests: `render_into` on empty and odd-sized slices does not panic and a poisoned mutex does not propagate (use `lock().unwrap_or_else(|e| e.into_inner())`); `feed` then `render_into` produces non-silence for a car at speed.
- [ ] Implement `AudioEngine` (AudioTrack MODE_STREAM, `AudioTrack.getMinBufferSize` x2, a daemon thread: `Native.audioRender(buf)` then `track.write`; `start()/stop()` idempotent; `setMuted` calls `Native.audioMute`), audio focus (`AudioManager.requestAudioFocus` with a listener that pauses/resumes), start in `surfaceCreated` after `gameStart`, stop in `surfaceDestroyed` and `onPause`, start again in `onResume` when the surface exists; mute button in the pause panel (`t("Sound: on", "Звук: вкл")` / off), state in `SharedPreferences("raytrace")` key `muted`.
- [ ] `cargo test --lib`; `bash tools/build-native.sh`; `./gradlew --no-daemon assembleRelease -q` compiles; commit `feat: audio glue and Kotlin AudioEngine`.
