# Tyres Implementation Plan (version 2.77.0)

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** Every tyre has a temperature, a wear and a pressure that decide its grip: it heats with the friction work, cools in the air, loses grip outside its thermal window and as it wears, and the player sets the cold pressure in the car menu.

**Architecture:** A pure module `game/tyre.rs` (heat balance, wear, thermal window, pressure and wear grip factors, per `wheel_set` and surface). `Vehicle` owns four `Tyre` states and multiplies the grip `mu_s` of each wheel by the tyre's grip; the world feeds it the ambient air temperature (from the sun, clouds and rain, `raster::smoke::Air::from_sky`) and the cold pressure (tuning part 12). The states reach Kotlin through the free stat slots 88 to 95; the car menu shows them.

**Tech Stack:** Rust (host tests via `~/bin/t`), Kotlin (UI, JUnit).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-21-driveline-design.md` (section 3.4, item 2).

## Global Constraints

- Branch `feat/tyres`; release `~/bin/rt-release 2.77.0`; versionName "2.77.0", versionCode 107; EN + RU changelog; release notes `~/projects/releases/rallynvx-2.77.0.md`.
- Never launch the game on the phone; never touch the real `profile.json`; report what tests cover and that the feel is unseen.
- 30 fps: no allocation in the vehicle substep.
- Tyres start **warm** (near the optimum of their set): the regression anchors (`anchors_tests.rs`: launch, braking, cornering) and the autopilot benches must hold unchanged; the effects show over a stage, in the heat of a hard drive, with wear and with a wrong pressure.
- Numbers (first guesses, in `tyre.rs`, to be tuned by the player's feel): heat capacity 5000 J/K per tyre; cooling `12 + 3.5 |v|` W/K; heat = friction work (`|Fx slip_x| + |Fy slip_y|`) + rolling hysteresis `0.012 Fz |v|`; thermal window `g_T = 1 - 0.125 ((T - opt) / half)^2` clamped 0.55..1 with (opt, half) tarmac set (85, 45), gravel (55, 50), snow (10, 35) degrees C; hot pressure `P = P_cold (T + 273) / 288`; pressure factor `1 - 4 ((P - P_opt) / P_opt)^2` clamped 0.7..1 with P_opt tarmac 2.1, gravel 1.6, snow 1.5 bar, cold default 1.69 / 1.41 / 1.53 (the hot pressure of a warm tyre is the optimum); wear `dW = friction work x abrasiveness / 2.5e7 J`, grip factor `1 - 0.25 W - 0.15 max(0, W - 0.8) / 0.2`; abrasiveness by surface asphalt 1.0, gravel 1.6, dirt 1.3, grass 0.6, sand 1.8, rock 2.5, water 0.5, times `1 + 3 (1 - tyre_factor)` for the wrong set.

## Review Focus

1. A car standing still or airborne: no heating, no wear, no NaN (Task 1 `a_standing_tyre_only_cools`).
2. A huge friction work in one step (a crash, a teleport): temperature and wear stay in range (Task 1 `abuse_keeps_the_state_in_range`).
3. The menu can send any pressure (0.8..1.25 of the default; NaN dropped): the grip factor stays 0.7..1 (Task 1, Task 3).
4. Wet and cold: the ambient temperature is low, the tyre cools but the grip never drops below 55 % from temperature alone (Task 1).

### Task 1: The tyre as a pure module
Files: create `rust/src/game/tyre.rs`, `rust/src/game/tyre_tests.rs`; modify `rust/src/game/mod.rs`.
Produces: `Tyre { temp, wear }` (`Tyre::warm(set)`, `Tyre::at(air_c)`), `Tyre::step(&mut self, h, heat_w, v, air_c, work_w, abrasion)`, `fn window(set) -> (opt, half)`, `fn grip(set, temp, wear, cold_bar) -> f32`, `fn hot_pressure(cold_bar, temp) -> f32`, `fn cold_default(set) -> f32`, `fn abrasiveness(surface, set_grip: f32) -> f32`.
Tests: warm tyre grips 1.0; cold tyre of the tarmac set grips about 0.7; hot tyre grips less; wear lowers grip monotonically; pressure above and below the optimum lowers grip (not below 0.7); a tyre heats with work and cools without; the balance temperature of a steady 8 kW at 30 m/s is 60 to 120 degrees C; wear grows with work and abrasiveness; garbage inputs keep the state finite and in range.

### Task 2: The vehicle's tyres
Files: modify `rust/src/game/vehicle.rs`, `rust/src/game/vehicle_tests.rs`.
`Vehicle.tyres: [Tyre; 4]`, `Vehicle.air_c: f32`, `Vehicle.tyre_bar: f32` (cold pressure multiplier, 1 = default). In the wheel loop: `mu_s *= tyre.grip(...)`; after the forces: the friction work and the heat step. Reset warms the tyres.
Tests: a hard slalom on tarmac heats the tyres above the start; a long cruise at speed cools towards the balance; a sliding car wears them; worn tyres give less lateral acceleration than new ones; a wrong cold pressure gives less grip; standing still the tyres cool; the anchors stay.

### Task 3: The world and the menu data
Files: modify `rust/src/game/assists.rs` (tuning part 12 `tyre_bar`, range 0.8..1.25, `TUNING_PARTS = 13`), `world.rs` (ambient temperature from the sky, the pressure), `app.rs` (`fill_state` slots 88..95: temperatures, wear), tests.
